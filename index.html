RailSentimentTriage bot

A personal project that finds sentiment information on websites about trains and rail infrastructure.

Politeness

  • Identifies as RailSentimentTriage/0.1 (+https://rail-sentiment-triage.github.io/) with a From header.
  • Follows robots.txt (RFC 9309). If it can't be reached due to a server error, the site is off limits.
  • At least 5 seconds between page loads, longer if Crawl-delay asks.
  • Pages that need JavaScript may be loaded in a headless browser with the same identity. It skips images, ads and tracking scripts.
  • At most 40 pages per site, at most 2 links deep.
  • Backs off on 429 or 503 and honors Retry-After.
  • Never logs in, submits forms, or tries to get past a CAPTCHA or block.
  • Skips AI analysis for sites that opt out (Content-Signal, Content-Usage, TDMRep, or robots.txt rules for AI crawlers). Never used for training.

How to block it

User-agent: RailSentimentTriage
Disallow: /
